更新日期 2026-09-30

保存在设备上的数据

游戏在当前设备保存关号、棋盘、爱心、用时、步数、道具次数、通关记录、已看引导、语言及声音和音乐偏好。关卡更新需要迁移时，可能在本机保留一次旧存档备份。用途是续玩、恢复设置和避免重复引导。

网络与第三方服务

无需注册账号，游戏进度和设置不上传给开发者。当前版本没有广告 SDK、统计 SDK、跟踪 Cookie、内购或支付系统；当前 iOS 版本不展示广告，网页体验版仅预留广告位置。音乐和图案随游戏提供，播放音乐不连接素材网站。打开版权来源链接或邮件应用由你主动选择，目标服务适用其自身政策。

客户支持

主动向liw58736@gmail.com发送邮件时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址及你选择提供的内容，仅用于回复和解决问题。请勿提供密码或无关的个人信息。邮件仅在处理问题及履行适用法定义务所必要的期间保留，你可通过该邮箱提出删除请求。邮件由 Gmail 处理，适用其自身政策，可能在其他国家或地区处理。

保存期限、访问与删除

本地记录保留到你使用“清除本机数据”、清理浏览器网站数据或卸载承载游戏的应用为止。设置中可以导出存档，也可删除本游戏的存档及迁移备份。导入存档会在你确认后替换本机当前进度；导出文件需由你自行删除。存档不跨设备同步；声音和语言可自行修改，当前关可重新开始。客户支持邮件相关请求请联系上述邮箱。

未成年人及安全

游玩无需提供个人信息，未成年人发送支持邮件时应由监护人协助。能够访问设备或浏览器资料的人也可能访问本地记录。删除数据或设备丢失后，无法保证恢复进度。

政策变化

以后加入在线服务、广告或支付时，将先按实际数据处理方式更新说明。页面日期用于标识本政策版本。

Privacy policy

Updated 2026-09-30

Who provides the game

Leafdoku: Logic Puzzles is provided by 英帅 王. Contact: liw58736@gmail.com. This policy covers the current local, offline gameplay version.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your level, board, hearts, time, move counts, tool uses, completed levels, tutorial history, language, sound and music preferences on this device. A previous save may be kept locally when a campaign update needs migration. These records let you resume play and avoid repeated tutorials.

Network and third parties

No account is required. Gameplay and settings are not uploaded to us. The current version has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking cookie, purchase or payment system. The current iOS release does not display ads. The web preview reserves space for future ads. Music and artwork are packaged with the game; playing music does not connect to the music website. Opening an external credit link or your email application is your choice and the destination has its own policy.

Support messages

When you email liw58736@gmail.com, we receive the address and information you choose to include, solely to respond and resolve your request. Do not include passwords or unnecessary personal information. Correspondence is kept only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by email. Gmail processes email under its own policies and may process it in other countries.

Storage, access and deletion

Local records remain until you erase this game’s data, clear this browser’s site data or uninstall the application containing it. Settings provides an export and a clear-data action, including the migration backup. Imported saves replace the current device’s progress after your confirmation. Exported files remain on your device until you delete them. Saves do not sync across devices. You can correct sound/language settings and restart a level yourself. For requests about support correspondence, contact the email above.

Children and security

No personal information is needed to play. A parent or guardian should supervise support messages from younger players. Local records are accessible to people with access to your device or browser profile. We cannot guarantee recovery after deletion or device loss.

Changes

If future versions add online services, ads or payments, their actual data practices will be described before those features are introduced. The date above identifies this version of the policy.
