续玩与重开

左上角返回首页会保存当前局，再点“开始”可续玩。设置中的“重新开始本关”只重置当前局，不会回到第一关。

道具和声音

已解锁道具点击立即生效，首次解锁各送 3 个，过关或重开不补回。当前 iOS 版本尚未启用广告补充。首次解锁会展示作用和规则。背景音乐与声音特效分别开关、分别调音量。浏览器需要先进行一次点击才可播放音乐，切到后台会自动暂停。

进度丢失

进度保存在当前设备。删除应用或清除游戏数据会移除进度；网页体验版的无痕模式或清理网站数据也可能导致丢失。更换设备前可在设置中导出存档，再在另一设备导入。目前没有云端同步或自动恢复服务。

Customer support

Contact

Email liw58736@gmail.com. Please include the level number, a short description and, if useful, a screenshot. Do not send passwords. We do not upload reports automatically.

Resume or restart

Return home to pause without losing progress; Start resumes your level. To restart only the current level, use Settings → Restart level.

Tools and sound

Unlocked tools work immediately when tapped. Each receives 3 items once; changing or restarting a level does not refill them. Refills by advertising are not enabled in this iOS version. Tutorial demonstrations appear at first unlock. Music and sound effects have independent switches and volume controls. The browser requires one interaction before playback. Music pauses while this page is hidden.

Lost progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app or clearing game data can remove it. Settings lets you export a save and import it on another device. No cloud sync or automatic recovery is currently offered.
